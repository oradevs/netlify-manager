# Changelog

Notable changes to Netlify Manager for Android. The current release on Google Play is listed on the
[store page](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.oradevs.netlify_manager).

## 3.0.0 – 2026-05-04

### Added
- Meta Audience Network as an additional ad provider on the free tier.
- Firebase Analytics for page views and purchase events.

### Changed
- Privacy policy updated to disclose the ad and analytics SDKs in use.

### Fixed
- Banner ads no longer reload too frequently on the free tier.
