Privacy Policy

Netlify Manager  ·  Last updated: May 4, 2026

Netlify Manager ("the App") is a third-party mobile client for the Netlify platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, how we use it, and your rights as a user. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

The App stores only your Netlify Personal Access Token locally on your device using encrypted storage. The App also uses third-party SDKs for ads and analytics that may collect limited device and app interaction data according to their respective privacy policies.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your Netlify token is used solely to authenticate API requests to the official Netlify REST API (api.netlify.com). All communication is directly between your device and Netlify's servers over HTTPS. We never read, log, or share your token.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data remains on your device:

  • Access token – encrypted at rest using Flutter Secure Storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • Tokens are never displayed in full or written to logs.
  • All API calls use HTTPS / TLS.
  • The App has no backend servers or databases of its own.

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party SDKs, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Netlify API – site management functionality. Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) – in-app advertising SDK. Privacy Policy
  • Meta Audience Network – in-app advertising SDK used for ad delivery and measurement. Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics – app usage analytics and event measurement. Privacy Policy

5. Advertising and Analytics Data

To improve app quality and measure monetization performance, the App may log analytics and ad-related events through integrated SDKs. Examples of events include page navigation and in-app purchase outcomes.

  • Purchase events – success, restore, and failure status with non-sensitive metadata such as product ID and event timestamp.
  • Navigation events – screen/page view events with page name and timestamp.
  • Advertising signals – device and app context data used by ad networks for serving and measuring ads.

We do not intentionally include sensitive personal data in custom analytics event parameters.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will take immediate steps to remove it.

7. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Delete your token by tapping Log Out in Settings.
  • Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data.
  • Request information about any data we may hold by contacting us.

8. Data Retention

The App stores your Netlify token only for as long as you remain logged in. Logging out permanently deletes the token from device storage.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be reflected in the App and on this page with a revised "last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, please contact us through the app store listing or our support email.

🔒 This app does not sell or rent your personal data to any third party.
